# Privacy Policy for Last Time

**Last Updated: January 13, 2026**

## Data Collection

Last Time does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information with third parties.

## Data Storage

All data you create in Last Time (tasks, completion dates, notes, photos, and reminders) is stored:
- Locally on your device
- In your personal iCloud account (if iCloud is enabled)

We do not have access to your data. Your information never leaves your control.

## iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is transmitted directly between your devices and Apple's iCloud servers using Apple's CloudKit service. We do not have servers and cannot access your iCloud data.

## Notifications

Local notifications are generated on your device for task reminders. No data is transmitted externally for notifications.

## Third-Party Services

Last Time does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
